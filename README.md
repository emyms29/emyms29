### [ccus](https://github.com/emyms29/ccus)
**What do people actually say about carbon capture?**

LDA topic modeling and sentiment analysis on about 2,800 tweets about CCUS. I found 5 topics, and people were more positive when CCUS came up alongside other clean tech. This one became my published paper.

`Python` `gensim` `spaCy` `NLTK` `t-SNE`

### [rt-iot](https://github.com/emyms29/rt-iot)
**Can a model spot an attack from packet timing alone?**

Nine classifiers trained on 123k IoT network flows. Tuned XGBoost won with 0.998 test accuracy, and the biggest warning sign was unusual gaps between packets. I presented it at the Stanford Pre-Collegiate symposium.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `Phi-K`
